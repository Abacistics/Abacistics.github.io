ABOUT ABACISTICS

Great engineering
shouldn't depend on
where you live.

Today, many robotics and engineering competitions require teams to travel, pay entry fees, buy specialized equipment, or belong to an organized school program. That leaves a lot of young engineers out.

Abacistics is built around a simpler idea: if you've made something, you should have a place to show it.
