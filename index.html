The Challenge: Maze Navigator

Your goal is to design a physical or coded solution that navigates through our official competition maze in the shortest amount of time possible!

How It Works:

  • Step 1: Build your maze navigator using any approved materials or code templates.
  • Step 2: Record a video of your project starting at the Entrance and clearing the Exit.
  • Step 3: Submit your timing results and video link through our submission form.
